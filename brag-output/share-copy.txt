BetweenUs is an all-in-one private communication platform engineered with zero media servers and true end-to-end encryption. From real-time messaging to collaborative voice rooms and outbound remote desktop, every byte is encrypted on-device and streamed directly between peers.

Highlights:
• True E2EE: Client-sealed AES-256-GCM encryption for messages, attachments, and calls—servers route ciphertext and never hold decryption keys.
• Zero-Media-Server WebRTC P2P: Direct device-to-device audio, video, and screen sharing with low-latency DTLS-SRTP encryption.
• Listen Together: Shared, synchronized YouTube queue in voice calls with real-time audio ducking when members speak.
• Play Together: In-call multiplayer board games including Carrom with authentic 2D rigid-body physics simulation.
• 24h Moments: Full-screen ephemeral stories with audience encryption keys frozen at post time.
• Outbound Remote Desktop: Reverse-tunnel desktop administration with zero open inbound ports and sub-20ms control.

Platforms:
• Desktop: Electron (Windows, macOS, Linux)
• Web: Modern React SPA
• Mobile: Native Android (Jetpack Compose & Material 3)
